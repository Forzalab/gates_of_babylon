# Scene A refs: analysis (Agent 1, before drawing)

Tony's 15 refs are in `refs/01–15`, numbered as in the plan's Context list. Palettes come from a Pillow 8-colour median-cut, with spot samples taken as 5×5 means.

**Numbering note.** The plan's example says "Refs 1 and 11: pink plastic box" and "Ref 10: kawaii faces and steam", but the files are the other way round:
- File **10** is the pink plastic box. It is byte-identical to `research/refs/pink-bento_0abe9d7b.jpg`.
- File **11** is the kawaii wooden 6-slot box.

So the pink box is built from **10 + 01 + 04**, and 11 only lends its gloss and sparkle language. Ref 06 is ref 05 with meme labels, used for the labels only.

## 01 · anime bento, tamagoyaki lifted in chopsticks (766×605)
- **Special:** the exact shot we need for the "lifted" state.
  - A tamagoyaki block is held in thin grey chopsticks from the upper left, over its own slot.
  - The rest of the box sits below, seen at a 3/4 angle:
    - broccoli
    - 2 cherry tomatoes
    - kinpira in a paper cup
    - a second tamagoyaki
    - 3 sausages
    - rice with nori-strip "eyes"
    - an **umeboshi dead centre on the rice**
  - The lifted piece shows its **cut face** (paler, with swirl lines) plus a seared top: brown blotches on yellow.
- **Palette:**
  - box pink `#d69eb4` (outer wall) / `#eabaac` (inside)
  - egg `#f7d87e`, egg lines `#eccc5f`, sear `#ad833a`
  - umeboshi `#c3514a`, rice `#eff6fc`
  - chopsticks `#c9b9aa`
  - background uniform grey-blue `#667c87`
- **Line:** thin dark-brown cel line (~2 px at source, ~4–5 px at 1080p). Two-tone cel shade with round white specular dots on the tomatoes and sausages.
- **Composition:** the diagonal of the chopsticks leads to the lifted food, upper left. The box fills the lower 2/3, and its near wall crops out the bottom edge. The lifted piece is the only thing above the box rim.
- **Use:** the layout of the lifted state, the sear blotches, the umeboshi sitting on the rice.

## 02 · tamagoyaki, anime (top) + photo (bottom) (401×765)
- **Special:** the **rolled-layer cross-section**.
  - Anime half: 2 rows of 4 slices, each end face a rounded square with a brown spiral line (3 turns), white flecks and a soft highlight.
  - Photo half: stacked slices with 6–8 thin concentric layers. The layers are **not** a clean spiral but nested, slightly squashed rounded rects, with a brown crust rim.
- **Palette:**
  - anime: `#fef9c3` highlight, `#f6d873` body, swirl `#8f7b64`/`#60453d`
  - photo: `#d7a94f` / `#caa24d` with a `#ce9641` crust
- **Line:** anime has no outer line on the slices, only the spiral stroke (1–1.5 px at source). The photo has soft shading.
- **Composition:** the slices are pressed shoulder to shoulder, so the cut faces read as a chain of blocks.
- **Use:**
  - the swirl, drawn as nested squircle layers that join into one spiral, lighter towards the core
  - crust ring darker
  - 3–4 white flecks
  - one highlight streak

| Rooftop noon | 05/06 | Trace + crisp fence, stairwell box, antenna, tiles, distant Figur tower. |
