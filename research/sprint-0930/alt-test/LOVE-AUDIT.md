# Love audit (PR #27, sprint/obbp)

Tony: "does each multiple choice path (inconsequential one included) all affect points? if not do it."

Audited every beat with 2+ options in the final applied scene graph (packs in play order, then `packs/love.json`): 46 choice beats, 15 reachable from scene 1 today (the rest are the old off-path scenes that stay in the data). Love goal 28.

Before: 10 beats had an option with no love value (0) or all options equal, all of them "move" picks (walk / open / climb / look). After: every option of every beat is non-zero and each beat has a positive and a negative option. Generated by `research/sprint-0930/alt-test/love-audit.mjs`; enforced by `src/date-beta-loveaudit.test.js`.

Rule used for the moves: the plain wording is -1 (she did not like that), the cute / obsessive wording (the heart or "... and nothing else") is +1. A fake pick (`walk-home:7` "Run home alone") plays option 0 and takes its value, so it is -1 too. Patched in `packs/love.json` (runs last, so beat numbers are final), via a new pack patch form `{scene, beat, choice, set}`.
