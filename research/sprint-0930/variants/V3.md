# V3 Kon: match cuts and one reality slip

**Pitch:** every place hands off to the next one by a shared shape (round bento → round sun, her hand on
yours → her hand on a basket, round plate → round station clock, train doors → umbrella, key → kettle knob).
Once, at the rain crossing, reality slips: every TV sign shows her face, and she tells you plainly why.

- Paprika rule, not Perfect Blue: the cut FLOWS. The beat after every match cut is a place/time stamp, often
  with "one hour later" said out loud, so the literal viewer is never lost.
- One day, noon to 7:10 PM. The clock is on screen at the station (4:30) as the match-cut payoff of lunch.
- Play: `?pack=story,meta,mech,lockgame,obbp,sequences,variant-v3` (pack: `src/date-beta/packs/variant-v3.json`).
  8 scenes after `rooftop`; rooftop's last pick goes to `v3-park`; hands off to the existing `cup` → endings.
- Shot ids from `SHOTS-SCHEMA.md` (sprint/seq-visual): `match` (iris on `of`, opens on `to`), `stamp`, `push`,
  `closeup`, `insert`. No new drawings. Optional: one `static` glitch frame of her face on the TV signs.
- Regenerate: `node research/sprint-0930/variants/gen.mjs v3`.
- Setups → payoffs: bento (round lid → basement bento boxes), three cups (shop rims → the same three cups at
  home, shown by a match cut back), curry (round plate → round clock), book (→ jars), key (→ kettle, door 12),
  slippers (kitchen), clock (station clock → basement chime).
- Loop (B5): the train-window reflection. Run 1: you and her. Run 2: "Same seat as last time." Run 3: three of
  you in a row.
- Illusion of control (B4): "Every road today came here. I planned all of them." Then the match cut proves it.
- Distinct shot types: match cut (8), stamp, medium, two-shot, insert, reaction, push-in, close-up,
  cut on action, J-cut, reality slip (pan up + static).
