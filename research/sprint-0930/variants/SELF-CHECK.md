# Self-check: where the grumpy literal teacher will still frown

Written by the variant writer, before judging. Honest list, not a defence.

## All three (shared problems)
- **Rooftop is untouched.** "Technically, rain wasn't f-{OR}-ecast" and "Stay f{OR}ever?" are word jokes. He will
  not get them. The stamp says "Rain is coming later" (V1) but rooftop's own lines still carry the joke.
- **Kept house/basement scenes are not rewritten.** `cup` has blank-text beats and "For Input B. Silly." He will ask
  "what is Input B?" `escape` jars/"names are all different" is fine; the lock game has no stamp ("lost minutes").
- **Time budget counts picks at 5 s but not the react line after each pick.** With ~12 reacts on the worst path, real
  time is ~5:50 instead of ~5:15. Still under 6:00, but only just. Blank `blackout`-style beats are gone from the date.
- **No stamp on `cup` (7:20 PM) or the basement.** Kitchen is stamped 7:10, then nothing. He may ask "what time is it
  now?" in the basement. The existing "a clock upstairs chimed" line partly answers it.
- **"A train is a big metal door. I hate doors."** Metaphor. He may say "a train is not a door". It is her logic (she
  fears anything that takes you away), but it is not stated.
- **Illusion of control reveal** ("Every road today came here. I planned all of them.") is literal, but "road" means
  "choice" here. He might read it as streets. Consider: "Every choice you made today led here. I planned them all."
- **Old dead scenes remain in the data** (park, errand-shop, town…). Not played, but the Tree/debug view shows them.
- **Town/crowd trick scenes are skipped** in every variant (the crowdwork {CROWD.n} lines). The lead may want to
  splice `town` back; it costs ~40 s.

## V1 Shinkai
- "Pull out. Two small people leave the park." He will ask "who are the small people?" It means you two, seen from
  far away. Say "You and Nanda, far away, leave the park."
- The time-lapse skies are the emotion. On a projector with text on top, he may just see "the sky changed colour"
  and feel nothing. Restraint reads as flat to him: the park and shop have almost no stated threat.
- "Silence. Wind moves the petals." A narrator saying "Silence" is odd to him (it is not silent, there is a voice).
- B2 escalation risk: V1 is the calmest; sweet → cracks is slow until the three cups (2 PM) or the train gate.

## V2 Yamada
- "My hand is shaking. Only yours can stop it." Clear. But "She is counting your pulse" — he may ask "why?" (it is
  possessive; not said). Might need "She counts your heartbeats. She wants to know they are for her."
- Many beats start "Close-up. Her feet…" Feet on a projector without new art are a zoomed crop of the background:
  there are no feet drawn. He will see a blurry road and read "her feet". This is the biggest build risk (needs the
  optional `feet` insert drawing).
- "She keeps the napkin." Creepy by implication only; he may not see why it matters.
- "Two days. I count those too." (run 2) Is it two days or the same day twice? The story says the same day repeats.
  He may be confused. Better: "Twelve stops. Second time today. You forgot. I did not."

## V3 Kon
- **Match cuts are the risk he names first.** "Her hand closes on yours. Cut. Her hand closes on a shop basket." The
  word "Cut" in narration is film jargon; he may not know it means "the picture changes". Each is followed by a stamp
  ("One hour later"), so he can recover, but the lead should test with "Now:" instead of "Cut.".
- **The reality slip** ("Every TV sign shows her face. Then the ads come back.") He will ask "is that real?" Her
  next line explains why (she wants to be everything you see) but not HOW. That ambiguity is the Kon point, and it is
  exactly what the rubric punishes. It is kept to one beat.
- "Three of you sit in a row. You, you, you." (run 3) Loop is literal, but the image may read as a horror glitch
  rather than "this is your third time". Consider "Three reflections. One for each time today."
- "Cut back" at the kitchen (three cups = shop cups) needs the viewer to remember the shop; library-branch players
  never saw the shop cups, so for them this match cut points at nothing. Needs a `vary.errand` fallback.
- Match-cut density (8) may feel like a gimmick by the station. Kon's own rule is flow; ours may be too regular.
