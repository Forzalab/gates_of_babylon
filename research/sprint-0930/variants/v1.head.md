# V1 Shinkai: light, sky, restraint

**Pitch:** the sky is the clock. Every place opens on the sky with a place/time stamp, time passes in two
time-lapse skies (train window, her street), and the scariest beats are silent holds, not speeches.

- One day, noon to 7:10 PM, then her kitchen. Weather order: clear → clouds → rain (5:30) → sun breaks → dusk.
- Play: `?pack=story,meta,mech,lockgame,obbp,sequences,variant-v1` (pack: `src/date-beta/packs/variant-v1.json`).
  The pack adds 8 scenes after `rooftop`, sends rooftop's last pick into `v1-park`, and hands off to the existing
  `cup` → `steeped` / `unknown` → `escape` → endings. The old date scenes stay in the data but are skipped.
- Shot ids (`props.shot`) come from `SHOTS-SCHEMA.md` on sprint/seq-visual. Until that merges, `bg` draws.
- Regenerate: `node research/sprint-0930/variants/gen.mjs v1` (data in `v1.data.mjs`; it validates with loadScenes).
- Setups → payoffs: bento (rooftop → basement bento boxes), curry (lunch → the kept naan/NAND joke), book
  ("you touched it once" → jars with names), key (held since noon → her door), slippers (her door), three cups
  (shop → three cups poured → "Input B" in `cup`), clock (every stamp → basement "a clock upstairs chimed").
- Loop (B5): the train line changes on run 1 / 2 / 3, same shot each time (Hosoda rule).
- Illusion of control (B4): every errand, food and pace pick ends at her door, and she says so: "Every road
  today came here. I planned all of them."
- Distinct shot types used: stamp, establishing pan, medium, two-shot, insert, close-up, reaction, hold/ma,
  pull-out, time-lapse sky, cut on action, exit. Each sequence uses ≥5 and ends on an exit shot.
