# V2 Yamada: intimate hands, feet, glances

**Pitch:** we watch her body before her face. Her feet set the pace of the day, her hands make every pick
physical (shaking hand, thumb on your pulse, nails in the basket, key marks in her palm), and her face
only fills the screen at dusk, when she has already won.

- One day, noon to 7:10 PM, then her kitchen. Weather order: clear → rain on the train (5:20) → rain crossing
  (5:30) → rain stops → dusk (7:00).
- Play: `?pack=story,meta,mech,lockgame,obbp,sequences,variant-v2` (pack: `src/date-beta/packs/variant-v2.json`).
  8 scenes after `rooftop`; rooftop's last pick goes to `v2-park`; hands off to the existing `cup` → endings.
- Shot ids (`props.shot`) from `SHOTS-SCHEMA.md` (sprint/seq-visual). Feet/hand inserts use `closeup` on the
  place art (zoom 2–3) and the `hands`, `key`, `umbrella`, `cups3` insert items. No new drawings needed; one
  optional new drawing: `feet` insert item (two pairs of shoes), used 5 times.
- Regenerate: `node research/sprint-0930/variants/gen.mjs v2`.
- Setups → payoffs: bento (rooftop → basement bento boxes), three cups (her hand, one-two-three → three cups
  poured → `cup` "Input B"), book (her fingers where you touched it → the jars), key (red marks in her palm
  → the lock clicks), slippers (she puts them on your feet), clock (stamps → the basement chime).
- Loop (B5): on the train she counts on her fingers: stops (run 1), days (run 2), three fingers (run 3).
- Illusion of control (B4): "Every road today came here. I planned all of them." She says it kneeling at your feet.
- Distinct shot types: stamp, establishing, insert (feet, hands, objects), extreme close-up, over-the-shoulder,
  glance / rack focus, two-shot, close-up (late), time cut, exit on feet or hands.
