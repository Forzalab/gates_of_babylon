# One decision = one scene: research (sequence writer, 9/30)

Tony's rule: every dating pick plays as a full little scene, not one line and a cut.

## 1. What a single film scene needs
- **Establishing (wide):** shows where and when, at the head of the scene. It lets the viewer process a new place.
  studiobinder.com/blog/types-of-camera-shots-sizes-in-film/ · pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6360176/
- **Medium:** two people in frame, body language + talk. **Close-up:** the face fills the frame, emotion leads.
  eac.libguides.com/c.php?g=723550&p=5190421 · adobe.com/creativecloud/video/production/cinematography/camera-shots-and-angles.html
- **Insert:** a tight shot of an object (the dish, the key, the cup) that gives the viewer a detail the wide shot can't.
  store.bandccamera.com/blogs/how-to/insert-shot-how-to-get-shots-that-are-important-for-a-movie
- **Reaction:** cut to the face after the event. Murch: cut for emotion first (brain file, film-research TL;DR 5).
- **Exit/transition:** wide again, or wider (Shinkai goes wide to extreme wide to end a scene).
  theregoesmykokoro.com/2017/04/09/thoughts-on-the-looks-of-your-name/
- **J/L cuts (split edits):** J = next scene's sound starts under the current picture; L = this scene's sound
  runs over the next picture. They replace fades inside and between scenes.
  adobe.com/creativecloud/video/post-production/cuts-in-film/l-and-j-cut.html · vimeo.com/blog/post/guide-to-film-cuts
- **Sound:** one ambience bed per place (room tone, crowd, rain) + spot SFX on the insert (sizzle, bell, pour).
  One sound = one meaning; silence 0.5–1 s before the scary line (brain file TL;DR 3, 6).
- **Ma (間):** a meaningful pause of no action; let the viewer see what the character sees.
  en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ma_(negative_space) · liamkhitaryan.com/embracing-silence-the-profound-impact-of-ma-in-anime-and-beyond/

## 2. Anime (Shinkai / Your Name)
- Heavy use of establishing and panning shots; every new place opens on sky/light first.
  artificialnightsky.wordpress.com/2017/04/08/your-name/ · studentfilmreviews.org/?p=38217
- Frames split by a line (door, rail, horizon) between two people. filmdaze.net/exploring-the-visual-themes-of-makoto-shinkais-your-name/
- Object inserts carry feeling (braid cord, phone, food steam); cuts land on motion (door slides, train passes).

## 3. Visual novels (our medium)
- A "shot" = bg + sprite pose + optional CG insert. VN cinematography = choosing which of those changes per click.
  youtube.com/watch?v=DW-RJrqHgn4 (Visual Novel Cinematography & Design)
- DDLC: hours of straight dating sim, then horror leaks in. Contrast is the tool.
  gamesinfocus.wordpress.com/2018/04/02/ (DDLC meta narrative)
- For us: new "shot" = same bg + `props.shot` hint (the art team builds the frame); narration lines = camera directions.

## 4. Yandere Simulator tone
- Generic slice-of-life school music with "disturbing" and "horrifying" variants of each track, by sanity.
  yanderedev.wordpress.com/2014/05/15/audio-design-and-music-demos/
- Tone = cute surface, wrong detail. For Nanda: the bed stays cute; one spot cue (tick, breath, drone) is wrong.

## 5. SCENE CHECKLIST (GAPS.md judges every decision on this)
| # | item | pass if |
|---|---|---|
| E | establishing | a wide beat names the place + light/time before any talk |
| M | medium / talk | at least one two-shot line where she talks to you |
| I | insert | one object close-up tied to the pick (the dish, the cups, the book) |
| R | reaction | a close-up of her face after the event, emotion fits her (love-bomb or flat psycho) |
| X | exit | a leave beat: wide again, mood line, J-cut sound of the next place |
| B | sound bed | an ambience cue on beat 0 of the place |
| S | spot SFX | a cue on the insert (sizzle, bell, pour, page, beep) |
| Q | ma | one wordless or near-wordless beat before the knife line |
| V | voice | every NANDA beat has a voice line in the voice script |
| F | fold | the sequence returns to the main path (no dead end, no loop) |
