## Common rules (applied to curry-katsu-dish / curry-katsu-cut / curry-katsu-pour)
1. **Crumb edge**: a scalloped bump path (r ≈ 10-16 px) all round the cutlet, plus a darker underside band.
2. **Highlights** on the roux come from the top-left: long orange streaks on the ridges and 2-3 small white blobs. On the crumb, a pale gold band sits along the upper edge.
3. **Pink cut face**: #f4b8b0 with a pale #fbe2d6 fat line, inside a 12 px crumb ring. The faces are angled toward the camera.
4. **Roux**: base #6a3414, ridges #8a4a1c, glints #e8862a, and a rim #3a1a08 where it meets the rice.
5. **Steam**: 3 soft S-curves (the Steam overlay), never a tween.
6. **Plate**: a white ellipse with a blue-grey inner shade at 3/4 top view. The rim is cut by the frame edge on the serving shot.
7. **Colour temperature**: warm food (gold, orange, brown) on a cooler neutral plate/counter, so the food pops. The pink cut face is the one cool-warm accent.
