# CURRY r2: the lines, rewritten at grade 2 (talk mode, literal)

The reader is a literal, autistic teacher, and the words are read aloud. So every narration line follows these rules:
- It says only what happens.
- It has no camera words ("Insert", "Close-up", "Extreme close-up"), because the picture already shows the shot.
- It has no figures of speech. "Her eyes never leave your face" was not literally true, so it is now "She looks at your face the whole time".
- Sentences are short, with one idea each.
- The time is said in words at the stamp beats.

**Voice takes are keyed by scene + words.** So the NANDA lines that have takes keep their exact words:
- 027 "I am hungry…"
- 031 "Feed me…"
- The pick reacts (028, 029, 030)

Those lines are already grade 2.
