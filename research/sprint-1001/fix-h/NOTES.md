- **Root cause:** `BentoLift` (art/scene-a/Bento.jsx) and `PovFood` (SceneA.jsx) drew two bare `<Chopstick>`s from off-canvas

- **Fix:** a new `HeldChopsticks` / `ChopHand` in art/scene-a/foods.jsx. It draws YOUR right hand (the MC's, so 5 fingers are OK; Nanda's

  grip, the thumb, index and middle sit on the sticks, and the nails are drawn. Beat 5: the hand enters from the lower left, and the box layout stays as on beat 4.
  Beat 6 (POV): the hand enters from the lower right, under the line of her sprite. On the tamagoyaki the sticks pinch it from behind (`behind`), so no
  stick crosses its face. No pack change was needed, and the change covers routes A, gacha, run2 and run3.
- **Files:** src/date-beta/art/scene-a/foods.jsx, Bento.jsx, index.js; src/date-beta/SceneA.jsx.

## H2: Nanda had five-finger human hands on the butter route (shop 3, shop 9, curry 6, 8, 12)
- **Wrong:** on "Her hand slides on top of yours" (shop 3), "Her nails press into the basket handle" (shop 9), "She tears off naan" (curry 6),
  "She dips the naan" (curry 8) and "She wipes your fingers" (curry 12 napkin), HER hand was a 5-finger human hand with nails. That breaks the PIN-hands lock.
- **Root cause:**
  - The shop JSX `Hand({her})` in art/shop/parts.jsx drew the same 5-finger hand for her, adding a pink sleeve and nails.
  - The curry pipeline (research/sprint-0930/curry/pipeline/draw.py) drew naan-lift, naan-dip and the butter napkin with `grip.hand(..., 'her')` / `hand(..., 'her')`.
  - Only the katsu shots used `pin_hold`.
- **Fix (the katsu approach copied):**
  - shop/parts.jsx: a new `PinHand` (the pin_hold look: a #d1177f rim lead with a #ffc4e6 lit core, a round nub, a sheen, grip ticks, and `press` strain ticks).
    `Hand({her})` now always returns it, so every shop call site gets a pin. Shop 3 (Street.jsx) moves the nub onto the back of YOUR right hand on the cart handle.
  - draw.py naan_lift: two pins. The anchor pin comes from the right edge and its nub presses the naan. The pulling pin comes from the left edge and its nub pinches the
    torn piece. Both leads stay out of the HUD band.
  - draw.py naan_dip: one pin from the right, its nub on the piece's held end.
  - The butter `napkin` now uses `napkin_shot(..., pin=True, scene='b')`, the same staging as katsu-napkin but graded with the butter tint.
  - The SVGs were regenerated with `python3 draw.py public/date-beta/trace/curry naan-lift naan-dip napkin`, these 3 only.
  - Before this change, naan-lift and naan-dip regenerated byte-identically from the pipeline. napkin.svg already differed from the pipeline output by about 200 B of
    upstream thali-sprite drift, so the regenerated napkin also picks that up. That is fine and was checked visually.
  - The Butter.jsx aria labels now say "pin hand".
- **Files:** src/date-beta/art/shop/parts.jsx, shop/Street.jsx, src/date-beta/art/curry/Butter.jsx, research/sprint-0930/curry/pipeline/draw.py,
  public/date-beta/trace/curry/{naan-lift,naan-dip,napkin}.svg.
