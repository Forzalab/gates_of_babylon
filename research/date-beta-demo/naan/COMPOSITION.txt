NAAN PLATFORM: composition notes from the 4 reference photos
=============================================================
Refs are BY-NC photos: viewed only, never committed (see CREDITS.txt). Numbers are fractions of
the frame (x across, y down) so they carry over to the 1920x1080 stage. Colours were sampled with
Pillow (box average) and then lifted to flat fills, so they match the flat rooftop / train scenes.

Stage rule for every variant: the dialogue box owns y >= 780 (box top 826, speaker chip 796).
The ad and the station sign must end above y = 780. Background (train, floor, fence) may run
under the box, the same way the rooftop railing does.


REF 1  09ef0c2f  elevated platform, rooftop billboards, train passing  (514x389, 4:3)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Camera: standing under the canopy, looking ACROSS the tracks, slightly oblique (to the left).
- Horizon: ~0.68 (roof line of the far platform canopy, 255-275 px). Everything important sits
  above it: the city is a band from 0.25 to 0.68.
- Vanishing point: off-frame left, on the horizon (the train roof and the far canopy converge
  gently to the left). Nearly frontal, so the billboards read as flat rectangles, no keystone.
- Canopy: dark underside fills the top-left triangle; its front edge is one strong diagonal from
  (0.65, 0) down to (0.25, 0.30). Exit sign + lamp + a purple LED light hang from it.
- Rhythm: overhead wires run in 3-4 parallel diagonals from top-right to lower-left; catenary
  poles and gantries stand on the far platform at ~0.12 W intervals.
- Billboards: on ROOFTOPS across the tracks, all in the 0.28-0.55 band. The biggest (red,
  portrait) at x 0.57-0.67. Small green + white signs further right at 0.80-0.95. They face us.
- Train: a long silver car with green/orange stripes fills the bottom 0.30 (0.70-1.0), moving
  left. At our stage size that band is exactly the dialogue zone, so the train can live behind
  the box and the ad sits above it.
- Light: dusk, sun just gone, behind and to the right. Warm pink rims on the right faces of
  buildings, violet shadow faces. Sky: violet-blue top, pink cumulus, clear #968cfa patches.
- Palette: sky #968cfa / #be80d7, cloud #e298d9, canopy #2b2a33, building #9f6269,
  red billboard #ca4e70, green sign #647c74, train body #6c435d (under shade).

REF 2  2d0e34e8  wide canopy platform, pillars, train on the right  (2048x1332)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Camera: on the platform, eye height, looking across it to the fence and city; a train
  stands along the right edge and recedes to the left of centre.
- Horizon: ~0.57 (fence top, far rooftops). VP: ~(0.40, 0.57), where the train's roof and
  sill lines meet behind the fence.
- Canopy: fills the top 0.25 at the centre (0.30 at the sides); heavy ribbed ceiling, a ladder
  walkway and a curved fascia beam that dips between the two pillars. Strong magenta cast.
- Pillar rhythm: two pillars frame the view, at x 0.18-0.23 (fat, near) and 0.60-0.63 (thin,
  further). The bay between them is the "picture": fence, grass, the city skyline.
- Billboards: none large; the bay between the pillars is the natural slot for a platform
  light-box (JP platforms put free-standing light-box ads right there, facing the platform).
- Floor: asphalt purple from 0.75 down; a yellow tactile strip runs across at ~0.92, and a
  second one runs along the train edge to the VP.
- Light: low sun from the right: the train's red/orange stripe glows, the pillars' right faces
  are pale pink, their left faces violet. Sky: deep violet top, pink-lilac at the horizon.
- Palette: sky top #482856 -> horizon #9b7fc7, canopy #561d44, pillar #6e4e7e, floor #391e4f,
  tactile (in shade) #6c3b5c, train body #8b638d, stripe #cb2859, city #686099.

REF 3  ea41d573  long perspective platform, hanging "2 Shinjuku" sign  (502x377)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Camera: on the platform near the edge, looking down its length. One-point perspective.
- Horizon ~0.65, VP ~(0.88, 0.65), far right. Every line (train roof, windows, canopy lights,
  tactile strip, gantries) runs into it.
- Train fills the left half, from full height at x=0 down to a point at the VP. A pink /
  magenta sky wedge sits between the train roof and the canopy edge (top-left quarter).
- Canopy: dark, over the right half; rows of lights recede to the VP. Pillars on the right.
- Sign: the foreground hero. A dark navy box hangs from the canopy, x 0.53-0.98, y 0.10-0.29:
  big white "2", then "新宿 / 千代田線 方面" and "for Shinjuku, Chiyoda Line". A green
  signal lamp hangs just below-left of it.
- Far wall / end: the platform ends in lights and a stair house at the VP. That end is where
  the eye lands, so the ad goes on the END FACE of a stair house at mid-depth (JP island
  platforms put ad panels on exactly those faces). All the perspective lines then point at it.
- Light: the sky is the only warm source (hot pink); the platform itself is cool
  (teal/navy lights). High contrast, dark foreground.
- Palette: sky #a864d9 -> #ff9ab0 near the horizon, canopy #2a2c31, train #36353d / #25415b,
  sign #305264 (navy), floor #3a4249, tactile #283e1c (dim yellow-green).

WHAT EACH VARIANT TAKES
-----------------------
V1  elevated platform (refs 1 + 4)
    - Ref 1's camera: across the tracks. Canopy diagonal top-left, exit sign, LED lamp.
    - Horizon at ~0.64 (y 690): the far platform canopy. City band y 300-690.
    - Hot NAAN = the big rooftop billboard across the tracks, frontal, lamps on arms above it
      (ref 4). Right of centre (ref 1 puts its red billboard at 0.57-0.67).
    - Sign: ref 4's white "1" box, hung from our canopy:
      「1 NAND-kano 中NAND / for NAND-kano, Mitaka」.
    - Train: ref 1's green/orange stripe car in the bottom band (under the dialogue box).
V2  canopy platform (ref 2)
    - Ref 2's camera and pillar pair framing the centre bay.
    - Hot NAAN = a free-standing light-box in the centre bay, at the back of the platform in
      front of the fence. The horizon (~0.57) runs behind it.
    - Sign: station name board (駅名標) hung from the canopy in the left bay:
      「池NOR袋 / Ike-NOR-kuro」.
    - Train: right bay, red/orange stripe, receding toward the centre bay (VP behind the ad).
V3  long perspective (ref 3)
    - One-point perspective computed from a small camera model (eye 1.6 m, f in px), so every
      line is true. VP pulled in from 0.88 W toward the centre so the stair house fits.
    - Hot NAAN = the end face of a stair house at mid-depth: the far wall. Every line points at it.
    - Sign: ref 3's navy hanging box in the foreground, top right:
      「2 新XOR / Shin-XOR-ku · Chiyo-DA Line」.
    - Train: left, receding to the VP; pink sky wedge above it.

Sign <-> variant mapping follows the refs: the "1 Nakano" box is from ref 4 (V1), the
"2 Shinjuku" box is from ref 3 (V3), so Ike-NOR-kuro goes to V2 as a station name board.

NIGHT (time="night", same geometry: the station after the blackout)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Every non-emitting colour goes through k() = nightify (navy ramp + 11% of the original hue), so dusk and night
share one drawing. Light sources skip k: tubes (with a halo), signs, lit city + train windows, exit sign, signal
lamp, vending machine, timetable panels. Rain: stepped (3 poses x 500 ms), clipped to outside the canopy, still
under reduced motion. Wet floor: faint strips of tube light on the floor (v2, v3), the vending machine mirrored
(v2), light pools on the far platform (v1). The ad: navy scrim (unlit) + NaanAd hold={NANDA_FRAME} (static).
Shots: V{1,2,3}-night.png and V{1,2,3}-dusk-vs-night.png (r3 left, night right).

REUSABLE FOR ALT'S NIGHT PLATFORM
---------------------------------
- Mount the whole station: import { NaanPlatform } from './NaanPlatform.jsx';
  <NaanPlatform variant="v2" time="night" rm={rm} />  (same place as the naan scene, by construction).
- Or build from stationParts.jsx: cam / sideQuad / flatQuad (the camera model), Sky (night stops built in),
  Blocks (city, lit windows at night), PerspTrain (lit windows at night), Rain, Tube, LineSign, NameBoard,
  Vending, AdSlot, nightify / tint. Canopy + pillar geometry lives in each variant (V1/V2/V3 in NaanPlatform.jsx)
  because it is the composition; the parts are the pieces that carry across.
