## 1. What the game is (so you can judge "does this fit?")

- A short visual novel (a dating game) for class, shown on **Thursday**.
- Anime style, like a Japanese school dating game.
- It **starts cute** (school rooftop, lunch, a date at a coffee shop). It **gets creepy** slowly (a train at night, rain, her apartment). It **ends as horror** (she drugs the tea, she won't let you leave).
- The girl is a "yandere": sweet at first, then obsessive.
- The **mood words** to keep in your head: *cozy, pink, rainy night, lonely train, too quiet, something is wrong*.
- It plays on a **classroom projector with ceiling speakers**, which sound like a phone speaker. So sounds must be clear in the middle range (clicks, taps, voices). Deep bass will not be heard.

## 3. Sound rules

- **Format: MP3 or OGG. No WAV.** (If a site only offers WAV, skip it, or tell Tony in Notes.)
- **Clean:** no music, no talking (unless the item is chatter), no hiss, no crackle.
- **One-shots** (a click, a thump): under 2 seconds, starts right away (no silence at the start).
- **Loops** (rain, train): **30 seconds or longer**. "Seamless loop" in the title is a bonus.
- **Phone test:** play it on your **phone speaker**. Can't hear it clearly? Pick another one.
- **Licence:** on freesound, use the filter **"Licence: Creative Commons 0"** when you can. Pixabay and kenney are fine as they are. Just copy the page URL, and Tony handles the rest.

## 4. Image rules (backgrounds + sprites)

**Backgrounds:**
- Anime / visual-novel style. **No photos.**
- **1920×1080 or bigger**, wide (16:9). In Google Images: **Tools → Size → Large**.
- **No people** in it. **No big text or watermarks** in the middle.
- Right time of day (see the item).

**Sprites / props:**
- **Transparent background (PNG)** is best. In Google Images: **Tools → Colour → Transparent**.
- A plain white background is OK too. We can cut it out.

**How to spot an AI image (skip these):**
- Hands with wrong fingers. Eyes that don't match. Earrings or buttons that are different on each side.
- **Text or signs that are gibberish** (letters that almost look like words).
- Things that melt into each other (hair melting into background, cups melting into a table).
- Too smooth, too shiny, "plastic" skin, and every image from the site has the same glossy look.
- The page says "AI", "Stable Diffusion", "Midjourney", "NovelAI", or it's from an "AI art" site.
- **Good signs:** a named artist, a pixiv/ArtStation/itch.io page with other hand-made work, a date before 2022, or it comes from a real game.
- **Not sure? Skip it.** There are plenty more.
