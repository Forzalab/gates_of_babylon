## 1. What the game is (so you can judge "does this fit?")

- A short visual novel (a dating game) for class. Anime style, like a Japanese school dating game.
- It starts cute. It slowly gets creepy. It ends as a horror scene.
- Settings: a school rooftop at sunset, a train at night, a rainy street, apartment stairs, a small Japanese apartment (kitchen, tea table), a coffee shop in the morning.
- It plays on a **classroom projector with ceiling speakers**, which sound like a phone speaker. So:
  - Sounds must be clear in the **middle range** (voices, clicks, taps). Deep bass will not be heard.
  - Mono is fine. Stereo is fine.

## 3. Licence rules (important, easy)

- ✅ **CC0 / Public Domain.** Best. No credit needed (still log it).
- ✅ **Pixabay licence.** OK. Log it.
- ✅ **CC-BY.** OK. You must log the author name and URL.
- ❌ **CC-BY-NC** ("NonCommercial"). Skip it.
- ❌ **"No redistribution"**, "personal use only", or anything paid. Skip it.
- ❌ **Any asset whose licence says "no AI" or "no AI tools".** Skip it. (We use AI tools in the build.)
- Not sure? Paste the licence text into Gemini and ask: *"Can a student use this in a free class game that is shown in public? Is credit needed? Does it forbid AI tools?"* If still unsure, skip it.

## 4. Quality bar (don't settle for "kind of OK")

**Sound effects:**
- Format: WAV preferred, MP3 or OGG OK. At least 44.1 kHz.
- **Clean:** no background music, no talking (unless the item asks for chatter), no hiss, no clipping (crackle when loud).
- **One-shots** (a click, a thump): short and tight, less than 2 seconds, no long silence at the start. Starts right away.
- **Loops** (rain, train hum): at least 30 seconds long. If the page says "seamless loop", even better.
- **Test:** play it on your **phone speaker**. If you can't hear it clearly, it will fail on the ceiling speakers. Pick a different one.

**Background images:**
- **Anime / visual-novel style** (painted or drawn). No photos.
- Size **1920×1080 or bigger**, 16:9 (wide).
- **No people, no characters** in the image.
- **No text, logos or watermarks.** (We add our own signs.)
- Matches the time of day in the list (sunset, night, rain, morning).
