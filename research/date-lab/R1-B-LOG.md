## Shared layer (src/date-lab/b/shared)
- `door.js`: the DOOR choice as a pure state machine. 5 s, 500 ms hold before a click counts, timeout = pink, replay = picked option disabled + timer still running (timeout then takes whatever is left). Both menus use it.
- `cues.js` + `audio.js`: 35 synthesized Web Audio cues (oscillators + one seeded noise buffer, no files). Nothing sounds before a click on the stage; `M` or the pill top-right mutes (saved in localStorage); every cue fires a caption (`CC [her breath, close]`) even when muted or locked. Beds (rain, hum, drone, tape, cicada, room, parade) loop; `muffle` lowpasses the whole mix for STEEPED.
- `ui.jsx`: stage root, sound chrome + captions, `<Line>` (OR rule: her red, 1px offset, breath SFX once per line with an OR), `useClock` (`?t=<s>` seek, `?pause`), `Markup` for the ported sprite.
- `art.js`: main's SVG sprite ported to an ES module; role colours are B's night tokens in `b.css`.
- `timeline.js`: shots + keyframed camera; reduced motion = hard cut to each keyframe at its time; `lint()` enforces >= 500 ms shots and <= 12 words.

## menu-5 · Circuit wires (horror 3)
**Theme:** "her wire is already soldered." Serial Experiments Lain (the Wired, power-line hum, PRESENT DAY · PRESENT TIME) on a Figur circuit board; her door is chip U12 (a 74HC00 = four NANDs).
**What it does:** the board boots trace by trace (8 fps). Her face is on a display module (DISP1) soldered to the board, cropped to an eye-line ECU with scanlines; her pink wire comes OUT of the screen from her hair-clip pin and is already soldered to the pink pad. The 5 s timer = her current crawling down that wire on the 125 ms grid; when it reaches the pad your wire is pulled there (default pink, "You didn't say no."). You drag a real jumper cable (sags, has a plug) from pin A · YOU; the drag lies: the plug bends toward the pink pad, more as time runs out. Near purple her face goes blank and the OR between the pads swells in her red with a sharper breath. Wiring purple = bleed, low bell, the board's traces go purple, her face goes wide/shadowed, "That's an OR. You know I hate OR.", she desolders it ("snip"), rewinds, and the replay has the purple pad burnt: red X, NC · LOCKED; dropping on it = dead-pad static and the wire springs back while the timer runs. Pink = all traces + U12's legs light pink, "The water's still warm." Keys 1/2, R.
**RM:** board boots in one cut, the current jumps once a second (5 stops), the wire snaps instead of springing, bleed = edge frame.
**Self-critique:** the cable path is a single cubic, so it can cross the U12 chip label; and the pull is invisible as a rule until you notice it (might need one tell, e.g. a faint pink magnetic field line).
**Round-2 carry-forward:** keep DISP1 (her face as a board component, wire out of the screen) and the lying drag; add a faint field line from the plug to the pink pad, route the cable around U12, and read the shrine circuit from Logic-mode localStorage onto the board as her "already soldered" design.
